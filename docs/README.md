# Docs

These guides are published, with the API reference, on the
[docs site](https://seanhudson.github.io/evalkeeper/). Edit them here:
`scripts/docs-site.mjs` copies each into the site when it builds, and
lists which page goes where. Screenshots come from
`npm run screenshots` in `examples/next`.

**Start here**

- [Try the example](try-the-example.md): every screen working, on demo
  models, in five minutes.
- [Concepts](concepts.md): what each word means, and the rules behind
  scores and suggestions. Start here if you're new to evals.
- [Before you start](before-you-start.md): what it needs, which parts
  your app uses, who it's for, and API keys.
- [What you get](what-you-get.md): the screens, the weekly check, and
  the ready-made suites.
- [Adding Evalkeeper to your app](adding-to-your-app.md): the steps, in
  order.

**Using it**

- [Build your answer key](answer-key.md): adding cases and confirming
  their answers.
- [Test models and choose one](choosing-models.md): running tests,
  reading the board, and making a model live.
- [Thumbs from your users](thumbs.md): ratings that become cases.
- [Keep the answer key useful](upkeep.md): the weekly check, and
  retiring cases every model gets right.
- [Best practices](best-practices.md): habits that keep tests honest.
- [Ready-made suites](ready-made-suites.md): prompt injection, crisis
  risk, and hopelessness, ready to use.
- [Models and prices](models.md): the catalog, and how costs are
  counted.
- [Write suites](suites.md): importing cases, special fields, decision
  models, and prompt injection.
- [Make the screens your own](screens.md): your components, and your
  own arrangement of a page.
- [Bring your own](bring-your-own.md): keeping your own eval tables,
  router, versions, thumbs, or pages, or moving in.

**Upgrading**

- [How to upgrade](upgrading.md), and [what changed](../CHANGELOG.md).

**How it's made**

- [Roadmap](roadmap.md): what's coming next, and later.
- [Architecture](architecture.md): how the packages fit, where each kind
  of change goes, and how it's tested.
- [Lessons](lessons.md): what went wrong in real use, and the rule each
  mistake left behind. New lessons go here (in the repo only); the advice
  for readers is in [Best practices](best-practices.md).
- [API reference](https://seanhudson.github.io/evalkeeper/api/): every
  export, with examples for the main ones.
